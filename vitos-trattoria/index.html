Since February 2024

Formerly Gamberetti's, still a neighborhood table

Vito's Trattoria serves traditional Italian recipes with a family-friendly atmosphere in historic downtown Albany. Indoor dining, outdoor seating, and a lively bar make it easy to turn dinner into a night out.

Owner-led hospitality

Alex Loomis, who managed Gamberetti's for five years, purchased the restaurant and opened Vito's to bring an authentic Italian dining experience to Albany. Catering and private parties are part of the mix — birthdays, reunions, and office gatherings.
