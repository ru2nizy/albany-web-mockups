Romantic dining, made to linger

Sweet Red describes itself as a small, family-run bistro — food fashioned for leisurely dining, plus handcrafted cocktails. Their site has called the room the valley’s most romantic dining for five years running.

Book ahead

Reservations are highly recommended. For same-day reservations, call the bistro after 3pm Wednesday–Saturday. Parties larger than six should contact the restaurant directly.
