What they sell

Menswear from casual to custom

Positioning pulled from thenattydresser.com — clothing, shoes, hats, grooming, jewelry, watches, colognes, gifts, and more.

Suits & made-to-measure

Premium and made-to-measure suits, plus custom suit consultations by appointment. Data-Fit shirts and pants for a closer fit without a fully bespoke process.

Tuxedo & suit rentals

Special-occasion rentals for weddings, prom, and black-tie. After-hours measurement appointments can be arranged when regular hours don’t work.

Everyday & accessories

Casual to business attire, shoes, hats, neckwear, grooming supplies, jewelry, watches, colognes, and gifts.

In the shop

Tailoring, alterations, and fittings

Services listed on their site — not just a clothing floor.

Alterations

Men’s, women’s, formal, casual, and military. They do not work on down-filled items. Appointments are required for wedding-gown and formal-dress consultations.

Custom clothier

Certified Custom Clothier through the Custom Tailors and Designers Association. Custom suits and vests for men and women.

More services

Custom shirts, bridal fittings, vintage shoe shines, hat repairs, and personalized letterman jackets — as listed on the official site.
