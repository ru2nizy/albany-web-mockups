Since 1980

A classic Albany diner with deep roots

Roger's Restaurant was established by Roger Seals in 1980. After decades of service, ownership passed to long-time servers Amy Guy and Jennifer Staten (sisters) and their husbands in 2019. The same warm, no-frills comfort food continues today.

Still family-run

The current owners worked the floor and kitchen for years before taking the reins. Many regulars have become like family. The goal is simple: keep serving Albany the same way for another 40 years.
