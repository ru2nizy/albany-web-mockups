Since October 9, 2001

The best of Oregon, made in-house

Sybaris is dedicated to bringing the best of Oregon to your table, served with equal parts eclecticism, classical preparation, and innovation. Owners Janel and Matt Bennett mark 25 years in 2026. The one employee with them since opening day is Jesse Brown.

From the chef

Everything is made from scratch unless a commercial product is better than what they can make. They bake bread daily. The pastry-chef background means desserts — including ice cream — are made in-house. Matt was invited to the James Beard House to cook a meal representative of the Northwest, and later returned for a Willamette Valley Native Tribute with The Confederated Tribes of Grand Ronde.

Highlights from sybarisbistro.com/menu — not a frozen price list. The kitchen works directly with small farms, ranches, and Willamette Valley wineries.

Dinner

Eclectic cuisine with flavors from around the world. A new menu each month. Current plates live on the official dinner menu — this concept does not invent dishes or prices.

Dessert & gluten-free

Separate dessert and gluten-free menus are published on the official site. Desserts use French chocolate, additive-free cream, and pure Hawaiian cane sugar, per the About page.

Wine & bar

An extensive Northwest-centered wine list and a full bar. Enjoy a cocktail with dinner in downtown Albany.
