Who we are

Beer, wine, cider — and a patio of carts

Positioning pulled from thebarnhs.com. The Barn Taproom has beer, wine, cider, and non-alcoholic drinks, with a food-cart hub outside.

Drinks

Over 42 beers on tap, plus wine, cider, and kombucha rotating weekly. Non-alcoholic options are part of the mix.

The patio

A large, covered outdoor patio with heated seating. The site lists 14 food carts offering all varieties of food.

Family owned

Family owned and operated in Hickory Station. Nominated for Willamette Valley Best of 2019–2024, per their About page. Kid and family friendly.

Check out our food carts

A large variety of food

Carts named on thebarnhs.com/food-carts. Lineups can change — this is what the official site listed.

Around the world

Los Vecinos (Venezuelan), Majahua Mexican Food, Rivas Dulce Vida (Salvadorian), Big Sisters Thai Kitchen, Ohana Kitchen (Hawaiian), Apollo’s Gyros, Dreaming of Sushi, and Kento Hibachi.

BBQ, crab, and American

High on the Hog (BBQ & boba), Kracked Crab, Cluckin’ Dog, The Chicken Shack, and Home Grown Oregon Foods (gluten-free, meal prep).

Sweets

Don Bigote for sweets and more. The homepage also flags desserts as their own stop: sweet and tasty treats.

We host private events

A room for up to 60

The Barn loves hosting private party events. Their private party room fits up to 60 people — rehearsal dinners, bridal and baby showers, family reunions, and business meetings, per thebarnhs.com.

Outdoor music

Outdoor music and other fun events are listed as part of what they do. Call for what’s on the calendar.

The space

Tables, heated outdoor seating, and music — theirs or yours on request. Vegetarian, allergy-friendly, and non-alcoholic options are noted for private events.

Book it

Use the contact form on the official site, or call the taproom. This concept does not invent an events email.
