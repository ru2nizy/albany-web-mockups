Why We Exist

Supporting Mercy House International

1st Hand Seconds is the fundraising arm of Mercy House International, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to helping women and their children break free from abusive relationships.

Your purchase has impact

All profit from the boutiques goes directly toward housing, resources, and support for women and children in our community who are leaving domestic violence situations.

What We Offer

Two Boutiques, One Mission

Donated inventory, carefully curated. Designer finds, classic pieces, and bridal gowns at accessible prices.

Main Floor

1st Hand Seconds Unique Boutique

An upscale resale clothing boutique in historic downtown Albany. Opened in 2010. Inventory is entirely donation-based — inspected, cleaned, and thoughtfully sorted. Known for quarterly dollar sales.

Women's clothing · Shoes · Jewelry · Accessories

Second Floor

Chicee Chicee Bridal

Appointment-only bridal boutique with over 300 gowns in sizes 0–32, plus veils, shoes, and accessories. Gently worn, never-worn, and vintage options. Average dress around $500.
