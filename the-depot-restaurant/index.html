Since 1976

A local institution in a historic building

The Depot opened in the old Trailways Bus Depot and has been serving consistent quality, fresh ingredients, and great service for nearly 50 years. Family-owned and still going strong.

What makes us different

We source local Oregon produce. Our fish comes from Alaska through an Oregon business. Fries are hand-cut fresh when you order. We bread the fish only after you place your order.
